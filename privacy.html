Snip & Keep privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026. Individual developer. Support: win212193@gmail.com. Public copy of this policy: https://win212191-wq.github.io/snip-and-keep/privacy.html.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores the text, URLs, page titles and screenshots you choose to save, along with notes, tags, reminders and audio recordings you create. Save selected text also keeps the pictures inside the selection; to do so it downloads those picture files again from the sites that host them. When you save or capture, the extension also reads what that page states about itself (such as product name, price, seller, author and date) to fill in the item’s table, on your device only. Audio recording starts only after you select Record and allow microphone access. Text recognition runs on your device using bundled models. To help diagnose problems, the extension also keeps its own recent error messages (up to 40 per part of the extension; they may include a page address) on your device only.

Optional website access

To keep saving and capturing across websites without reopening the extension, you can choose Keep using across websites and approve Chrome’s optional all-sites access prompt. Without it, temporary access via the toolbar remains available. Page contents are saved only when you request Save or Capture. You can revoke website access in Chrome’s extension settings. Chrome settings and protected pages remain unavailable.

Free trial and license key

Snip & Keep can be used free for 7 days after installation. After that, saving new items needs a license key bought from our payment company, Polar (polar.sh; purchases are handled on its website under its own privacy policy). When you register a key, the extension sends only the key and a computer label (for example “Snip & Keep · Windows”) together with our public seller ID to Polar’s license service to confirm it; while online, a registered key is checked again about once a week, and releasing a key sends the key again to free that computer. The trial start date and the registered key are kept on your device. No saved items, pages, screenshots, notes or recordings are sent.

Optional AI with your own key

In “Edit into a document” you can choose to use AI to summarise or tidy up text. This is off until you enter your own API key from an AI company you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) and agree, and Chrome asks you to allow that company’s address. Only when you press an AI button, the text of that block (or, for the whole-document summary, the document’s headings and text) is sent directly from your computer to that company with your key, and its reply is put into your document. Pictures are not sent. The developer receives nothing; the company handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy, and use is billed to your account there. The key is kept only in this browser’s extension storage, is not included in backups, and can be removed at any time in the AI settings.

Transfers and sharing

The extension has no developer server, cloud account, analytics or advertising. Apart from the license key check and the optional AI requests described above, nothing is sent to an outside server. Your content is not sent to the developer or sold. Opening a source link connects to that website. You control any files that you export or share.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. Items in Trash can be restored; permanent deletion cannot be undone. Removing the extension or browser profile may delete stored data. Exported backups contain unencrypted content and should be stored securely.

Limited use

Information accessed through Chrome APIs is used only for the collection, viewing and organization actions you request, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
