A personal scrapbook in Chrome's side panel: save text, pictures and pages as you browse, group them by topic and find them again.

Save while you read

  • Selected text with its pictures and source link
  • Whole pages
  • Screen captures: visible area, selected area, full page
  • Notes, voice recordings, reminders

Collect by topic

  • Everything you save goes into the topic you are collecting for
  • Topic notes, filters, favorites, search
  • Restore from Trash

Use it again

  • Read text in pictures (Korean and English), on your device
  • Export to Excel or PDF
  • Full backup and restore

Price

Free for 7 days after installing. After that, one payment of $9.99 keeps it working — no subscription. When the trial ends only saving new items stops; everything you saved can still be opened, edited and exported. Payment and license keys are handled by Polar, the payment company. One key works on up to 3 computers.

Privacy

What you save stays on your computer. There is no account, developer server, ads or analytics. The only thing sent out is your license key and a computer label when the key is checked. Read the privacy policy.
